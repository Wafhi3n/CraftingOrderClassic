# Crafting & Gathering Order — Classic — CurseForge description

> Source of truth for the CurseForge page. Copy it onto the addon page on each notable update.
> (Not packaged; see `.pkgmeta` ignore.) Kept short on purpose (2026-10-10, user): a player should get
> it in 15 seconds. The full feature list lives in `CHANGELOG.md` and the in-game Help tab.

---

The game never tells you who can craft what. This addon shares your professions with everyone else
running it, so you can find a crafter and order from them, guildmate or stranger.

- Hover an addon user to see their professions, then right-click to order.
- Order crafts or gathering from everyone, your guild, your friends or one player.
- Tick "Announce in Trade" so players without the addon see it too: `WTB [item] x1 2g50s #CO27`.
- Pick "Whole guild + Discord" to post it in your guild's Discord channel (your guild needs Discord
  linked with "Separate Discord chat from Guild chat" ticked).
- In the profession window: incoming orders, the cheapest way to level, and where to get missing
  plans.
- Accepted orders stay on screen like quests, with reagents counted from your bags.

Type `/co` to open it, `/co help` for every command.

WoW: Forever (1.60.1) only. Era, Season of Discovery and Hardcore stay on 1.30.0. English, French,
German and Spanish.
